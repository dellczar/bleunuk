Our Story

Rooted in the Caribbean,
Made in Brooklyn

Bleu Nuk BK is where Jamaica meets Trinidad and Tobago, right in the heart of Brooklyn. Every dish is crafted with fresh herbs and bold spices — the kind of cooking that comes straight from the heart. Pull up a chair, let the music find you, and stay a while.
